Terms of use

The rules and limitations of using the Flowkenstein BPMN canvas.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The service

Flowkenstein is a free, open-source tool for drawing business processes in BPMN 2.0, available without an account. It maps processes; it does not execute their tasks or certify business or regulatory compliance.

Your content

You retain the rights to your diagrams, subject to third-party rights. Creating them with Flowkenstein transfers no rights to the publisher. Make sure you have the necessary rights and permissions for content you use or share.

Saving and backups

Automatic saving stays in browser storage. It can be lost if you clear that data, use another browser or device, change the website origin, or if storage is unavailable. Regularly save a BPMN copy using “Save as…”.

Image exports are intended for presentation. Keep the BPMN file to continue editing a diagram.

Imports and limitations

Imported files are checked to protect your browser. Oversized, overly complex or malformed files, or files with unsupported extensions, may be rejected. Some features or extensions from other software may not be reproduced.
