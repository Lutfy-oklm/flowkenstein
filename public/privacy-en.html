Privacy policy

Your diagrams stay in your browser. Here is how other data relates to the operation of this website.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Controller and contact

The publisher of the personal Flowkenstein project is responsible for the processing they carry out. Contact: ttdesigny@gmail.com. Details about the publisher and host are in the legal notice.

Your diagrams

Names, tasks, connections and other diagram content are processed in your browser. The application does not upload them to a server or send them to the publisher.

Automatic saving uses local storage to restore the diagram and its view the next time you open the canvas. This data remains on the same device, in the same browser and for the same website origin until replaced or deleted. The application does not encrypt it.

Imported BPMN files are read locally. BPMN, PNG and SVG exports are saved on your device. Exported copies remain under your control.

Deleting local data

In your browser settings, clear data for flowkenstein.com to remove its local save. “New diagram” replaces the current diagram. Export your work before taking these actions. Downloaded files must be deleted separately on your device.

Hosting and technical data

To deliver pages, maintain availability and protect the service, Vercel may process technical data such as your IP address, browser information, request date and URL. Local diagram content is not part of these requests.

This processing serves the legitimate interest of delivering and securing the website. Retention is limited to delivery, diagnostics, security and applicable legal requirements; the provider’s retention criteria are described in Vercel’s privacy notice. Flowkenstein does not maintain an additional visitor database or personally identified browsing history.

Vercel is based in the United States and uses international infrastructure. Processing may occur outside the European Economic Area. Applicable mechanisms and safeguards are described in its data protection documentation.

Cookies and browser storage

Flowkenstein does not install advertising or audience-tracking tools in its pages. Local storage saves your diagram and view. You can block or clear it in your browser; automatic saving may then stop working.

Storage strictly necessary for the requested service is exempt from prior consent. Adding non-exempt tracking in the future would require appropriate information and consent.

Contact messages

If you contact the publisher, your email address and the information you provide are used to respond to your request, based on the legitimate interest of providing contact and support. These exchanges are handled in the publisher’s email service, provided by Google, and are not published on the website.

Messages are retained for as long as needed to handle and follow up on the request, then deleted when no longer useful, subject to applicable legal requirements. Avoid sending sensitive data or diagrams containing confidential information.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions of the GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure or restriction of processing, object to processing based on legitimate interest and, where applicable, exercise the right to data portability. Contact ttdesigny@gmail.com. The publisher cannot access diagrams stored only in your browser.

You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL. This policy will be updated if features or processing activities change.
