Getting started

One idea. Three simple steps.

1

Set the scene

Open the canvas and double-click lane names to rename your participants.

2

Draw the journey

Choose shapes from the toolbar. Add your steps, then connect them with arrows.

3

Keep a copy

Choose “Save as…” in the menu for a BPMN file, or “Export image…” for a picture.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the canvas and start drawing right away.

Where is my diagram saved?

In this browser’s local storage. Your diagram comes back when you reopen the same canvas. Save a BPMN file for an independent backup: clearing browser data also removes the local save.

Can I open an existing diagram?

Yes. Choose “Open” in the canvas menu and select your .bpmn or .xml file.

How can I share my process?

Export a PNG or SVG image to present it, or save the BPMN file so someone else can continue editing.
